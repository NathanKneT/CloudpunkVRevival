# (`perf supersample 2`, no MaxEyeResolution cap) to load the GPU. Then the load is lightened (supersample 1.3):
# a step up only waits during play and is taken when the pause menu opens.
#
# Check: "Render resolution: eye ..." lines stepping down within a few seconds (70% per side at most at 90 Hz), and
# no "no frame reached SteamVR" or "showed an old frame" warning. With the lighter load the Performance line says
# "step up waiting for a menu or a black screen" until the menu opens. The "perf <label>:" lines give the GPU time
# at each stage.
