# The HOVA's mirrors (dev command `mirrors`; MirrorQuality, MirrorAngle, RearMirror).
# The game feeds both dashboard screens from one blurry 64x32 camera on the left flank. The mod draws each screen's
# own side (256 px across at Low, 512 at High) and adds a rear-view mirror hanging from the ceiling beside Camus.
# `mirrors look left|right|rear|strip [close]` turns the test head to one, `mirrors snap <name>` saves the textures
# to BepInEx\mirrors-<name>-*.png. A mirror more than 80 degrees off where the head points isn't drawn.
# In order: every setting in turn, captures at Game, Low and High, Camus poked into the mirror, the cost at rest, what
# occlusion culling hides, the screens' width, then flying with banks and hovering at a few cull radii.
#
# Check:
#  - "draws 64x32 for both screens", then "each rear-view screen shows its own side", and after the charm is placed
#    "Mirrors: the rear-view mirror hangs ... beside Camus on his left" with "side by side" and "the district sign
#    shows whole above it". "Camus not placed" is a failure.
#  - "Mirrors: the rear-view mirror looks out from N m behind the HOVA's centre" (N about 2 to 3) and "the game's
#    occlusion culling leaves everything within 25 m drawn".
#  - the `charm` lines after each poke: "the rear-view mirror beside him, knocked N time(s)", N going up.
#  - "perf mirrors-*" lines: off is the floor, game the reference. "perf mirrors-low" should stay within 0.5 ms CPU of
#    "perf mirrors-game".
# Captures:
#  - 48-game-*: both screens the same blurry picture. 48-low-*, 48-high-*: sharp, each screen its own side, upright.
#  - 48-rear-*: the mirror in its dark metal frame left of Camus, the street behind mirrored, no HOVA body in the
#    glass. Dark sky, never white or flat grey ("plain, darkened"); *-gamelook-* is the game's look, for comparison.
#  - 48-knock-*: Camus swings into the frame and stops there, never through it.
#  - 48-fly-rear-bank, 48-fly-low-left-bank: the horizon stays level in the glass while the HOVA banks (it tilts in
#    the game's own, 48-fly-game-left-bank).
#  - 48-hover-cull* and 48-gdoc-*: what culling hides behind you at each radius, and with GDOC off.
