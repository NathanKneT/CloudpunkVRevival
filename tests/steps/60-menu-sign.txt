# The relight starts once the menu is in view, during the menu camera's glide (60-glide-*). `sign at <s>` holds the
# relight at s seconds so the frames are the same every run; `sign relight` is what F11 does; `sign snap` writes
# close-ups without bloom and fog to BepInEx\sign-<name>-line.png and -sign.png.
#
# Check:
#  - "Menu sign: VREVIVAL built under the game's CLOUDPUNK", 8 letters, layer 31; no "missing", "sound none" or
#    "not as expected"
#  - eight "Menu sign letter" lines, letter 6 with "flickers: U-off under the game's Flicker layer"
#  - "Menu sign: relight 1 started" then "relight 1 done", all 8 letters lit after about 4.15 s
#  - "Menu sign check 1: drawn by 'MenuCamera' in both eyes" (flat: its single view), 8/8 letters in each eye, about
#    as many passes per eye; "whole in view", clouds and cab not in front. "FAILED", "IN FRONT" or "NOT whole in
#    view" are failures (the waitlogs match either way so the run goes on). Same for check 2.
#  - relights 2 and 3 log the same timeline as relight 1
#  - "Menu sign: off (MenuSign = false)" then "on again (MenuSign = true)"; in the game, `sign` says "in the menu's
#    view: no"
#
# Captures (60-*.png: both eyes; 60-*-mirror.png: the game's window):
#  - 60-sign, 60-sign-clean (menu card hidden), 60-relit: VREVIVAL in the same neon as CLOUDPUNK, stepping down to the
#    right like it, in front of the cloud, ending before the cab; same glow, same in both eyes. The 6th letter may be
#    caught dim: it flickers like the U of PUNK.
#  - 60-relight-0.50 to 3.50: the relight held at each time (0.50 all dark, then left to right, the 6th V failing once
#    and catching last). 60-noflicker: the 6th V lit like the others. 60-off: the game's sign alone; 60-on: back.
#  - BepInEx\sign-60-lit-line.png: close-up, no seams, edges as clean as CLOUDPUNK's.
#  - 60-ingame: nothing of the sign in the game.
