# and `vrsettings set wheel`). Start with the HOVA at rest in the cockpit. Sweeps are numbered ("wheel sweep #N") so
# each waitlog waits for its own. Sweep angles are the wheel's; it goes back to where it started after each.
#  1. `wheel physics`: what the game steers with, and where the HOVA starts to turn (6 deg of wheel at any speed with
#     lock 35); every "wheel:" line says "turns from 6.0 deg".
#  2. Both hands sweep the wheel to 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30 deg, 3 s each. At rest the law is linear: 5 deg about
#     1 deg/s, 10 about 34, 15 about 78, then the game's cap.
#  3. The presets through the VREVIVAL menu's own apply and save: 50, 65, 25, then 35 again (sweeps #2 to #5). At 65,
#     10 deg hardly turns the HOVA; at 25, 30 deg is past the stop.
#  4. `wheel sweep compare`: a longer lock never turns faster for the same wheel.
#  5. Square with both hands on the wheel changes nothing: no "VRevival menu" line, `wheel lock` still says 35.
#  6. At speed: wheel at its stop, full throttle, the HOVA spirals out instead of flying straight. Sweep #6 holds 35,
#     30 and 25 deg and checks the offset against the HOVA's physics.
#  7. A capture at the 35 deg stop, both hands on the rim.
#  8. The free wheel follows the stick with the same gearing, and grabbing it then doesn't make it jump.
#
